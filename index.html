What changes here?
Physical overlap can change. Two 60° cones can overlap differently in the room when their camera positions change. The textbook does not give enough camera-position information to calculate that physical overlap uniquely.

2. Geometry view for #14 Angular coverage: same directions, translated to common vertex O.
Why introduce point O?
Point O is not another camera. It is a mathematical reference point. We copy only each camera's direction and 60° FOV width to O. Once all three angle intervals share one vertex, their angular overlap can be measured directly.
What #14 is actually measuring
The given information is sufficient to determine total angular coverage, not the exact physical area seen by the cameras. Camera distance is therefore ignored in this lower model.
